# Renal Impairment Review Protocol

1. Confirm baseline renal function is documented (SCr, eGFR, BUN). If missing, flag as
   "Not addressed in provided references" rather than assuming normal function.
2. Cross-check the patient's renal risk factors (pre-existing renal insufficiency, diabetes,
   age > 65, volume depletion, concurrent nephrotoxic drugs) against any risk factors listed
   in the drug label's own warnings — call out explicitly which of the label's listed risk
   factors this patient has.
3. Check home medications for nephrotoxic agents (NSAIDs, aminoglycosides, other nephrotoxic
   drugs) and flag them as modifiable risk factors if present.
4. Recommend a renal-function monitoring schedule (baseline, during, and after treatment) —
   do not state specific numeric thresholds unless they come from the reference material.
5. Note hydration/volume status considerations if the drug label mentions them.
